Themes generated from logos: prototype results

Every colour below is computed in this page from the logos' pixels by the proposed engine (docs/adaptive-theming-plan.md): analysis in OKLab, roles, OKLCH scales, three variations in light and dark, the contrast fixer and the colour-vision check on charts. Status colours (on track, at risk, overdue) are the fixed ones and never generated. The two sample logos are invented.
